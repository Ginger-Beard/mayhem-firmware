# Signature Detect signature class: DECT 6.0 (US) cordless phone bases, energy
# only. One custom sweep band, 1916-1930 MHz (below the PCS downlink at 1930).
# Format: /SIGDET/README.TXT. valid=real: carrier seen in a test recording;
# valid=doc: from documents only.
#
# An idle base sends one beacon of ~90 us every 10 ms on one of five carriers
# (1.728 MHz apart), 1.152 Mb/s GFSK, ~1 MHz wide: about 1 % duty. The sync word
# was checked offline in the recording; the device sees energy only (low
# confidence, '?'). points=180 gives short looks (51 us) and more of them, so
# a beacon can fill a look. A rule needs energy in 2 looks of a dwell, in 2
# dwells: expect seconds to tens of seconds near a base. duty=0-20 rejects a
# continuous carrier or spur. No burst= / period=: one look is far longer
# than a beacon. A base in ECO / no-emission mode is silent when idle.
# NOT YET TESTED ON THE DEVICE (rules written 2026-11-14 from the sweep code).
