# Zigbee (802.15.4) channels 20 and 25, energy only: Zigbee PRO frames
# were decoded on these channels (and 15) in test recordings. A Wi-Fi burst
# or a BLE connection on the same frequency reads the same (low confidence);
# listed after hits in 2 dwells (dwells=). Channel 15 (2425 MHz) is left out:
# at 276 kHz points it cannot be told from BLE advertising channel 38
# (2426 MHz), which every phone nearby fills (54 "Zigbee ch15?" on one drive).
NB,label=Zigbee ch20,kind=home,f=2450,bw=2000,db=8,duty=0-40,valid=real
NB,label=Zigbee ch25,kind=home,f=2475,bw=2000,db=8,duty=0-40,valid=real
# Z-Wave frame receiver: checksum-valid R1 frames (9.6 kb/s) on 908.42 MHz, seen
# 2026-11-12 on a test network (real). It takes a
# 1.5 s dwell in each cycle; the NB rules below are the energy fallback. The
# 908.4 energy rule is tied to it: listed only while a Z-Wave network has
# been decoded in the last 10 min (any 908 MHz ISM traffic reads the same).
ZW,label=Z-Wave,kind=home,rate=9k6,f=908.42,valid=real
